About Langa Konnect

Langa Konnect is a community-driven directory and events hub built to connect residents, small businesses, organisations and volunteers across Langa. Our goal is to make it simple to discover local services, join events, support small vendors, and promote community-led initiatives. Anyone from the township can add a business, list an event, or share a local resource — we encourage respectful, accurate listings that help the whole community thrive.
